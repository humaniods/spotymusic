Choose New releases to load the Audius catalog.

No payments, accounts or premium unlocks inside OpenMic. Music is provided by Audius, Openverse, Wikimedia Commons and Radio Browser. Click each song’s source/credit link for licensing and attribution. Online radio is a live broadcast, not song-on-demand. A free API does not guarantee every popular commercial release is available.
